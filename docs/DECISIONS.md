# Decisions

Decisions that docs/GDD.md does not cover. The Phase 4 to 8 prompts read this file.
Open decisions and their recommended defaults are listed in the last section of docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md.

## Settled

| Date | Decision | Answer | Needed by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-13 | Which phone, and is there a Mac? | Web build as planned: a TypeScript web game installed to the phone's home screen. No App Store release, no Mac needed. | Phase 0 |
| 2026-11-13 | How is the "core and sim import no rendering code" rule enforced? | A Vitest test (`tests/architecture.test.ts`), plus a second type check of `src/core` and `src/sim` with no DOM library (`tsconfig.core.json`). No ESLint. | Phase 0 |
| 2026-11-13 | Which random number generator? | sfc32, seeded from a hashed string, in `src/core/rng.ts`. `fork(label)` gives each subsystem its own repeatable stream. `Math.random` is banned in `src` by a test. | Phase 0 |
| 2026-11-13 | Which versions? | Node.js 26 LTS. Phaser 4.2.1 (current stable), Preact 11.2.0, Vite 8.3.8, Vitest 5.1.0, TypeScript 7.0.2, all pinned exactly. | Phase 0 |
| 2026-11-13 | How is the controller read? | The browser's Gamepad API directly (`src/game/gamepad.ts`), not Phaser's gamepad plugin. | Phase 0 |
| 2026-11-13 | Start Phase 1 before the Phase 0 controller test? | Yes. The Phase 0 page is still at `?controller`. No `phase-0` tag until the test passes with a real controller. | Phase 1 |
| 2026-11-13 | Keyboard controls? | Yes, as a fallback for checking things on a computer: arrows or WASD skate, Space shoots, Shift sprints, Tab opens tuning. Feel is judged on the controller. | Phase 1 |
| 2026-11-13 | Units and step | Metres and seconds. The simulation always advances 60 fixed steps a second; a fast puck is moved in shorter sub-steps so it cannot pass through a post or the netting. | Phase 1 |
| 2026-11-13 | Can the puck be carried into the net? | Yes, it counts as a goal. Revisit when goalies arrive in Phase 2. | Phase 1 |
| 2026-11-13 | Start Phase 2 before the controller tests? | Yes, at the owner's request. Existing skating values were left alone; only new values were added. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | How do humans and AI share the game? | One set of mechanics. The AI produces the same input a controller does and cannot do anything a player cannot. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | What does a rating of 70 mean? | It reproduces the plain physics values. Every player starts at 70 until Phase 4 generates real players. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | Opponent difficulty | One number from 0 (easy) to 1 (hard), `game.opponentDifficulty`, starting at 0.5. It changes reaction time, aim, passing awareness, checking and shot selection, never physics. The AI teammate plays at a fixed 0.6. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | When does control switch? | To the puck carrier whenever your team has it; to the receiver the moment you pass; on defence, Y jumps to the skater nearest the puck (or the other one if you are already on him). | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | Who can collect a loose puck? | The intended receiver of a pass; anyone close enough if the puck is slow enough to handle; an opponent standing in the lane of a flat pass. A puck too fast to handle deflects off bodies. A saucer pass cannot be touched until it lands. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | Stoppages | None yet. A goalie who freezes the puck holds it for a second, then lobs it to his most open teammate. After a goal everyone resets and the puck drops loose at centre. Faceoffs and periods come in Phase 3. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | Can the puck be carried into the net? | Yes if the goalie is out of position, but a goalie smothers a puck carried into him. Replaces the Phase 1 answer. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | How many players on the ice? | 3 skaters and a goalie per side (the GDD first said 2 skaters). `docs/GDD.md` has been edited to match. | Phase 2 |
| 2026-11-13 | How many players are dressed? | 6 skaters + 1 goalie: two lines of 3, from the roster of 7 skaters + 1 goalie, so one skater stays home. `docs/GDD.md` has been edited to match. This overrides the "4 skaters and 1 goalie" and "two lines of 2" wording in the Phase 2, 3, 4 and 5 prompts of `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | How does a line change work? | Skaters skate to their bench door on the top boards; each is swapped for his opposite number as he gets there. The puck carrier goes once he no longer has the puck. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Who calls line changes? | Automatic for both teams: when the line on the ice is tired, the bench is fresher and the moment is safe (the team has the puck, or the puck is in the other half). LB forces a change at once. At a faceoff the bench line starts if it is clearly fresher. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | How are faceoffs decided? | A timing duel. The puck drops after a random wait; the first centre to press A after the drop wins it back to a winger. Pressing early locks that centre out for half a second. If nobody presses within a second the puck lies loose. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Fatigue | Two bars. The sprint bar is unchanged. Each skater also has shift energy that falls on the ice (faster when sprinting) and refills on the bench; below 60% it cuts speed, shot power and check strength. Goalies tire slowly over the game and react a little later. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Does the clock run through stoppages? | No. It runs during play and stops for the goal celebration and the faceoff set-up, so a goal does not eat a 2-minute period. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Overtime | Sudden death with the same lines, played until someone scores. The balance harness alone caps it at 5 minutes and counts a tie. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | What counts as a shot, a save, an assist, a hit? | A shot is any shot attempt. A save is a goalie stopping a puck that an opponent shot (loose pucks and passes do not count). Up to two assists go to the teammates who had the puck before the scorer, as long as the other team did not have it in between. A hit is a body check that lands. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Can a goalie be skated around? | A goalie smothers any puck carried within 1.2 m of him. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Default opponent difficulty | 0.75 (was 0.5), because a scripted player that only skates to the slot and shoots beat level 0.5 easily. Balance targets are measured AI against AI at 0.5. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Aim assist | The skater the human controls gets it; the AI does not. A shot pointed within 50 degrees of the net is steered to the corner further from the goalie; pointed anywhere else it is left alone. Strength, cone and corner inset are in `physics.json` under `aimAssist`. Added because aiming at the net from a keyboard's eight directions was too hard. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | Pass assist | For the skater the human controls only. Between teammates in the direction you point, one with a clear lane is preferred over one with an opponent in the way (a saucer pass ignores the lane). With the stick centred, the pass goes to the teammate with a clear lane and the most room. It never picks a teammate outside the aiming cone. Values are in `physics.json` under `passAssist`. | Phase 3 |
| 2026-11-13 | What do F and D mean? | A tendency only. Any three skaters can form a line. Forwards are generated with better shooting, defencemen with better checking; when two teammates support the puck carrier, the forward goes ahead and the defenceman trails. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Backup goalie? | None. One goalie per team; goalies tire but are never injured. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Roster size | At most 7 skaters and 1 goalie. To sign when full, release first. A team needs 6 skaters and a goalie dressed to play. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Cost of releasing a player | Nothing for now: he goes to the free-agent pool and his cap hit comes off at once. Revisit when contracts and cash matter (Phases 7 and 8). | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Grade bands | S 95+, A+ 92 to 94, A 88 to 91, B+ 83 to 87, B 78 to 82, C+ 72 to 77, C 65 to 71, D under 65. The GDD pairs (A+ / A and so on) are each split in two. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Cap hit | Follows overall in a straight line through each grade's range from the GDD: S $11M to $13M, A+ $9.5M to $11M, A $8M to $9.5M, B+ $6M to $8M, B $4M to $6M, C+ $2.75M to $4M, C $1.5M to $2.75M, D $0.8M to $1.5M. Rounded to $50,000. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Overall rating | A weighted average of the ratings. Forwards weigh shooting most, defencemen checking, goalies reflexes and positioning. Weights are in `src/data/overallWeights.json`. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | The league | Your team plus 12 AI teams whose average overall runs from about 66 to 80, and 24 free agents from D grade up to one A. Your team averages about 68. Everything is rebuilt exactly from the league seed. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | Who do you play? | Any AI team, chosen from a list, until the season run arrives in Phase 5. Wins, losses and overtime losses are kept. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | New signings and the lineup | A signing fills an empty lineup spot if there is one; otherwise he waits undressed and the game says so. | Phase 4 |
| 2026-11-13 | How long is a season? | 3 regions, each a game, a stop, a game, a stop and a boss (with a choice of two at every step but the boss), then a semi-final and a final, each best of 3: 13 to 15 games. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | What does a loss cost? | Three strikes. A lost map game is a strike and you move on; a lost boss game is a strike and a rematch; the third strike ends the season. A lost playoff series ends it outright. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Powerups and artifacts | Powerups are consumables: load one on LT and one on RB before a game; pressing the trigger uses it up for a timed boost or an instant effect. Artifacts are always-on team bonuses kept across seasons until prestige. The AI does not use powerups; bosses have a signature trait instead. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | What does a season reset? | Powerups, run currency, training bumps and temporary effects are wiped at season end. Artifacts, the roster and the record carry on. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Roster during a season | Locked. The dressed six skaters and goalie are fixed for the run and the seventh skater stays home; the six can still swap lines. Signing and releasing happen only in the off-season. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Stops on the map | Game: a win pays 20 currency plus 5 per goal of margin (up to 15 more), with a 35% chance of a powerup. Training: one dressed player gains 3 in every rating for the season. Event: a text choice with a trade-off (18 written). Shop: three powerups for sale and a strike wiped for 120. Boss: a generated team 3 points stronger than its region's best, with a trait; a win pays 40 and a choice of three artifacts. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Who are the opponents? | Region 1 draws from the four weakest AI teams, region 2 the middle four, region 3 the four strongest; the playoffs are against the second strongest and then the strongest. The AI is sharper each region (0.5, 0.65, 0.8; bosses 0.1 more; playoffs 0.9). The tuning panel's difficulty shifts all of these together. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Champion's prize | One artifact, awarded automatically. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Can an event end the season? | No. Events can add or wipe a strike but never deliver the third; currency never goes below zero. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Quitting mid-game | The season is saved after every step, but a game in progress is not: quit during a game and you replay it. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Exhibition games | Still available in the off-season against any team; they no longer count toward the record. | Phase 5 |
| 2026-11-13 | Who owns gear? | The team. A piece is assigned to a player and returns to the inventory when he is released. Gear never changes a player's grade or cap hit. It can be changed at any time between games, in or out of season. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Milestone targets | Scaled to the season, each once per season and each a Gold pack: 4, 8 and 12 wins; reaching the playoffs; winning the championship; a player with 12 goals; 3 team shutouts; a hat trick. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Goalie gear | Three slots: mask, leg pads, blocker. Skaters have six: stick, skates, gloves, helmet, pads, jersey. About one piece in six from packs is goalie gear. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Gear strength | Each boost is worth 2 points on a Common, 3 on a Rare, 4 on an Epic and 5 on a Legendary, plus 1 per level above the first, up to level 5. Levelling costs tape; scrapping an unworn piece pays 5, 15, 40 or 100 tape by rarity. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Set bonuses that the game could not do as written | Sniper 4 ("charged one-timer"): a fully charged shot is 20% harder, as there are no one-timers. Enforcer 4 ("big hits knock the puck loose"): checks land much more often. Burner 4 ("breakaway speed boost"): 8% faster while carrying the puck. Playmaker 4 ("passes thread through sticks"): his flat passes cannot be intercepted or blocked by opponents. Brick Wall is as written (the first shot that beats the goalie each period is saved) but comes at 3 pieces, since goalies have 3 slots. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Which pack does a win earn? | One pack per season win: Silver for a shutout, a win by 4 or more, or a boss win; otherwise Bronze. Milestones add Gold packs on top. Exhibition games earn nothing. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Pity timer | If 24 packs in a row hold no Legendary, the 25th has one in its guaranteed slot. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-13 | Duplicates | There is no automatic duplicate handling: any piece not being worn can be scrapped for tape. | Phase 6 |
| 2026-11-14 | Skill points on level-up | Placed automatically where the position values them (shooting for a forward, checking for a defenceman, reflexes for a goalie), never past the player's hidden potential. No allocation screen. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | What earns XP? | Skaters: ice time, goals, assists, shots, hits, blocked shots. Goalies: saves and wins. The skater left at home earns 30% of the average dressed skater. Players aged 25 or under earn 25% more. XP is banked and levels applied when the season ends. Each level is 3 rating points and costs 15 XP more than the last, starting at 100. Exhibition games earn none. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | When does a cap hit change? | Only when a contract is signed. A player who improves mid-contract is a bargain until renewal; one who declines is overpaid until then. Re-signing costs what his grade is worth at that moment, for 1 to 4 seasons. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Aging | 26 to 30: nothing. 31 to 34: speed, stamina and (goalies) reflexes fall 1 to 2 a year. 35 and over: those fall 2 to 3, passing and positioning 0 to 1, everything else 1 to 2. Once a player is declining he has no more room to grow. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Retirement | Never before 35. Then 12%, 20%, 32%, 45%, 60%, 80% a year, and certain at 41. His gear returns to the inventory. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Expiring contracts | Yours that reach zero must each be re-signed or let go before the next season can start. The rest of the league does not move until you have decided, so a player you let go is in the pool when AI teams shop. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Can AI teams sign players you let go? | Yes. The off-season news says who went where. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | How do AI players improve? | They play no simulated games, so by a yearly rule: below their potential they gain 6 to 16 rating points a year to age 25 and 0 to 4 to age 29. Players on your team who are not yours do the same. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Rookies and the pool | 12 rookies aged 18 to 20 join the pool each off-season, overall 56 to 72 with 4 to 24 points of room to grow. The pool is kept to about 36, always with affordable skaters and goalies in it. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Trades | Off-season only, one player for one player of the same kind (skater or goalie) with any AI team. The AI values a player at his overall, plus half his room to grow, minus 1.5 for each year past 30, and accepts if it gets at least as much as it gives. Both teams must stay under the cap. You are told whether each of your players would be accepted before you offer him. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Prestige | Offered in the off-season once you have won the championship at your current level, up to level 6. Keeps the roster, contracts, gear, tape, levels and ratings; wipes artifacts. Each level: every opponent plays 2 rating points better and its AI is 0.04 sharper; Rare, Epic and Legendary odds rise 15% (Common never below 20%); one jersey colour unlocks. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Jersey colour | Seven colours, one to start and one per prestige level. It changes your skaters' colour on the ice and nothing else. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Blocked shots | A shot by the other team that deflects off a skater's body counts as a block for him. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Multi-season health check | Games are settled from team strength rather than simulated, so 400 franchise-seasons run in a second. The real simulation is covered by its own tests and the Phase 5 season run. | Phase 7 |
| 2026-11-14 | Can the mascot replace the goalie? | No, skaters only. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Can a mascot drop-in be declined? | No, and the skater who sits is picked at random. The game screen tells you who before you play. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Medical wing without injuries | It affects fatigue only: each level, your players tire 4% slower and recover 4% faster on the bench. There are no injuries. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Home and road games | Games alternate, starting at home. It only decides money: tickets at home, travel on the road. Nothing changes on the ice. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | When money moves | After each game: tickets or travel, merchandise, a playoff gate at home. When a milestone is reached: its bonus. At the end of the season: sponsorship in; players, staff, upkeep and the mascot out. Building and costumes are paid at once. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Where most income comes from | Sponsorship, 18% of franchise value a season. Seasons vary from 4 to 15 games while salaries do not, so income that depended on games played bankrupted every style of manager. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Franchise value | Starts at $100M. Each season it moves by half the profit or loss, $0.4M a win and $8M for a title; each facility level adds $1.5M when built. Never below $20M. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Fire sale | With cash below zero in the off-season the next season cannot start. Until it is back to zero, releasing a player under contract pays half a season of his cap hit and letting a staff member go pays half a season of his salary. Anyone who joined this off-season, or whose contract has run out, pays nothing. With nothing left to sell, the league covers the debt and the franchise loses a tenth of its value. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Do AI teams have money? | No. Only you run the second budget. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Staff | Two trainers, a scout and a promoter, hired from a pool of 10 that is new each off-season, graded D to S ($0.3M to $3M a season) on 1 to 3 year contracts. They can be hired at any time and are paid at the end of each season. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | What "a trainer boosts XP in one skill area" means | XP is per player, not per rating, so each trainer works with a group: shooting coach for forwards, defence coach for defencemen, goalie coach for goalies (5% more XP per grade step), skating coach for everyone (2%). | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Facilities | Six, levels 0 to 5. Level n costs $3M x n and each level costs $0.3M upkeep a season. Effects per level: training centre 6% more XP and 8% less aging; medical wing fatigue; front office 3% off re-signing; scouting office 1 scouting point; arena 8% more tickets; mascot den 1 mascot power. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Scouting | Potential is shown as a range that always contains the true value: 16 points wide, 1.5 narrower per scouting point, 3 narrower per season a player has been with you. Players aged 31 or over show "past his peak". With no scouting 60% of the free-agent pool is visible, 5% more per point; the hidden ones are those with the most room to grow, never the cheap ones, and cannot be signed. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Mascot | One per franchise, costing $0.5M a season. Power is costume level (five levels, $2M x level) plus the mascot den plus a little from a promoter. Drop-in chance is 5% plus 3% per power, capped at 35%, fixed by the season seed and game number. He plays at 88 overall plus 0.75 per power, earns no XP, never ages, and his goals count toward no milestone. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Rival mascots | Each AI team has one, with power of half a point for each point its average is above 66. Bosses have power 4, 6 and 8 by region. Same drop-in rule. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Talents, as the game can do them | All trigger by themselves, only while he is on the ice. Crowd surge: after his team scores, the team skates 10% faster for 10 seconds. Spin move ("undodgeable deke"): the first check that would strip him fails; recharges in 12 seconds. Distraction: when he carries the puck into the attacking zone the other goalie reacts 50% slower for 10 seconds; recharges in 40. Cannon shot: his fully charged shot is 30% harder. Rally cry: once a period, when his linemates' energy is under 45%, it is refilled. One talent to start, another at power 3, 6, 9 and 12. | Phase 8 |
| 2026-11-14 | Difficulty | Rookie, Pro or All-Star, chosen when a franchise starts and changeable between seasons. It shifts every opponent's AI level (Rookie -0.2, Pro 0, All-Star +0.15) and nothing else: ratings, money and rewards are the same on every level. Older saves are Pro. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Simulating a game | Ordinary map games can be simulated instead of played; bosses and playoff games cannot. The result comes from team strength with your team counted 4 rating points weaker, so playing is the better bet. XP, cash, packs and strikes are as for a played game; powerups are not used. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | The spare skater | Between games the seventh skater can be swapped in for a dressed one. Training bumps stay with the player who earned them. This replaces "the seventh skater stays home all season". | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | The hub | Four groups (Team, Front office, Collection, Settings) under the primary actions, instead of one list. Staff and facilities are locked until the first season is finished, so the first run is not a wall of systems. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | What the run currency is called | Pucks. Every screen carries the same strip: cap space, cash, tape, and Pucks while a season is on. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Rewards screen | After every season game, played or simulated, a screen steps through the result, cash and Pucks, XP for each player with a bar toward his next level, then any pack and milestones. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Real set bonuses | Sniper 4: a shot let go within 0.5 seconds of receiving a pass is a one-timer, which counts as fully charged and is 20% harder. Enforcer 4: a body check at a closing speed of 5 or more always knocks the puck loose. Burner 4: 12% faster with the puck when no opposing skater is between him and the net. Playmaker 4 was already the design doc's effect. These replace the Phase 6 stand-ins. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Look | "Arena scoreboard": dark navy, ice-white text, one amber accent, bold capital headings in the system's own narrow fonts. No image or font files: menus are HTML and CSS, the rink is drawn in code. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Team colours | Each team's jersey colour comes from its name out of a palette of 14, and is never close to the colour you are wearing. Goalies wear a lighter shade. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Touch controls | A virtual stick on the left; Shoot, Pass, Check, Swap and Sprint on the right; powerups and line change along the top. They appear on the first touch and step aside when a controller or keyboard is used. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Left out of this round | League standings, tighter money, and a first-run guide: not chosen. | Design round |
| 2026-11-14 | Team crests | Every team has a crest worked out from its name: one of four badge shapes, one of eight emblems, its main colour, a second colour that always stands out against it, and a white or dark border. Shown in the menus, on the scoreboard and at centre ice. Your crest wears the jersey colour you chose. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Jersey designs | The main colour always dominates so the two teams read apart at a glance; the second colour is an accent worn as an outline, a band or shoulder patches. A first version that coloured the whole shoulders made two teams with the same second colour hard to tell apart. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Arena | Stands and a crowd around the rink, with pockets of each team's colours mostly behind its own net; they sway, and the scoring team's fans jump on a goal. Advert panels on the boards and four made-up sponsor names painted on the ice. The rink is drawn about 7.5% smaller to make room above and below. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Effects | Skating stride, a lean into speed, skate marks that fade, snow on hard stops and hits, a streak behind a hard shot, a ring on hits and posts, goalie pads down on a save, a red light behind the net and a banner that slides in on a goal, a short screen shake on goals and big hits. Effects only watch the simulation; they never change it. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Sound | Every sound is synthesised in the browser from recipes in `audio.json`: no sound files. One-off sounds come from the simulation's events; the crowd and skates are beds that run during play. Sound starts after the first key press or tap, which browsers require; a controller button alone may not count. Sound on or off, volume and rink detail are device preferences, kept apart from the franchise save. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Pack opening | A sealed pack, then its cards face down, turned one at a time with a glow in the rarity colour, or all at once. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Motion | Screens settle in, bars fill, the focus changes smoothly. All switched off when the device asks for reduced motion. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Not done from the plan | Numbers counting up on the rewards screen, a sweeping stick, and the net bulging on a goal were planned and not built. The closer camera was not chosen. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Skating sound | Removed after the owner listened: the constant skate noise was unwanted. The other sounds stay as they are. | Graphics round |
| 2026-11-14 | Hub | A dashboard: a team card, one large button for the next thing to do, and the four groups and Exhibition as tiles in a two-column grid, each with an icon and its live status. The d-pad moves in all four directions on this screen only. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | Icons and gear art | Every menu row has a drawn line icon. Every kind of gear has a drawing, framed and tinted in its rarity colour, in the gear list, the gear card, a player's slots and the pack-opening cards. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | Player portraits | Every player has a face worked out from his id: head shape, skin tone, hair style and colour, brows, eye colour, and for some a beard. It never changes, except that hair turns grey at an age fixed per player (from 32 on) and the youngest have no beard yet. He wears his team's colours; a free agent wears neutral ones; the mascot has his own head. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | Title screen | The game opens on a title screen with the working title, your crest and where your franchise stands. The title and tagline are in `src/data/game.json`, so the name can change in one place. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | End-of-game screen | Both crests, the score, and the three stars of the game: skaters scored by goals, assists, shots, hits and blocks, goalies by saves, goals against, a shutout and the win, with a little extra for the winning side. A player who did nothing is never a star. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | Leftovers finished | Cash, Pucks and XP count up on the rewards screen; the net bulges for half a second on a goal; a skater's stick sweeps with his stride when he does not have the puck. | Interface round two |
| 2026-11-14 | Season screens | The map's stops are cards (a game shows the opponent's crest and strength against yours); the game screen is a matchup poster; the shop, training, events and the season's end each have a drawn layout. Strikes show as three lights. | Interface round three |
